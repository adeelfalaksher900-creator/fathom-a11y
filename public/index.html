        How it works

        See it. Hear it. Fix it.

        01

        See through other eyes

        Preview your page with physically-based color-blindness matrices, blur, glare and tunnel-vision lenses, then switch to a keyboard-only view that numbers every tab stop and flags clickable things the keyboard can never reach.

        02

        Hear what's really there

        Fathom approximates a reading-order transcript from DOM semantics and reads it aloud. Unlabeled buttons, file-name images and anonymous form fields become impossible to ignore. Compare it before and after with ElevenLabs voices.

        03

        Fix it, and learn why

        Every finding names the people it affects, the WCAG 2.2 criterion and a plain-English reason. One click applies a safe fix and re-audits live. Inclusive-language and reading-level checks make the words as welcoming as the code.

        Why we built this

        Inclusion is a feature you can measure.

        Technica exists to make tech a safe harbor for people who are too often left out. The same is true of the products we build. Fathom turns “make it accessible” from a vague promise into a score, a list, and a button, so a student building their first site can ship something everyone can use.

        • 1 in 4adults in the US lives with a disability (CDC)
        • 1 in 12men have some form of color-vision deficiency
        • 95.9%of top-million home pages have detectable WCAG failures (WebAIM Million 2026)

        Fathom was built solo for Technica 2026. AI disclosure: designed and written with Claude (Anthropic). Voices by ElevenLabs. Audits run locally. Embedded remote resources can contact their hosts; optional live voice sends transcript text to ElevenLabs.

        The score covers automated checks, not WCAG conformance. Review suggested fixes. Always test with real assistive technology and real people.
